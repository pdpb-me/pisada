# Pisada — exercise catalog (Phase A)

Draft · 14 Oct 2026 · source: `claude/Ash_Month2_Program.md`

These are the 8 non-video exercises in Month 2. Each entry is what the figure must show and what the card text says. Dose (sets, reps, hold) lives on the session, not here. Reviewed with Pedro on 14 Oct; open questions resolved.

**Shared conventions**
- **Frames:** static, drawn left to right as one strip. Two frames by default, one for holds; a mid frame is added only where the movement needs it (none currently do).
- **Highlight:** the working area is in the accent colour. For foot exercises that's the **right** foot and calf.
- **Arrows:** one arrow per frame transition, showing the direction of movement.
- **"Don't" icons:** at most 2 per exercise. Each is a small crossed-out mini figure, shown in the larger view, not on the thumbnail.

## Neck micro-dose (seated at the desk)

### 1 · `chin_nod` · Chin nod
- **Setup:** sitting tall, back and back of the head resting on the chair back.
- **Frames:** ① neutral, eyes forward → ② small "yes" nod, chin in, back of head slides up the chair. Hold 5 s.
- **Cues:** Small "yes" nod · Head stays on the chair · Jaw relaxed
- **Don't:** chin poking forward · hands on the head
- **View:** side · **Highlight:** upper neck

### 2 · `t_hold` · T-hold
- **Setup:** sitting tall, away from the chair back.
- **Frames:** ① single frame: arms out wide at shoulder height, thumbs up, chest forward, shoulder blades back and down. Hold 15 s.
- **Cues:** Thumbs up · Shoulders down and back · Chest forward
- **Don't:** shrugging · head poking forward
- **View:** front, plus a side inset for "chest forward" · **Highlight:** shoulder blades (in the inset)

### 3 · `thoracic_ext_chair` · Thoracic extension over the chair back
- **Setup:** sitting, top of the chair back at the bottom of the shoulder blades, arms crossed on the chest.
- **Frames:** ① upright → ② mid-back bends back over the chair edge, head moves with the chest.
- **Cues:** Bend over the chair edge · Head follows the chest · Slow
- **Don't:** hands on the head · looking up at the end
- **View:** side · **Highlight:** mid-back

## Tendon block

### 4 · `ball_squeeze_heel_raise` · Ball-squeeze heel raise (wk 1–2)
- **Setup:** standing, small ball or rolled towel squeezed between the heels, fingertips on a wall.
- **Frames:** ① flat feet → ② full height, ball still squeezed. Tempo 3 s up · 3 s down.
- **Cues:** Squeeze the ball the whole time · Straight up · Weight over the big toe
- **Don't:** heels drifting apart · rolling onto the little toe
- **View:** side, plus a foot inset from behind showing the ball between the heels · **Highlight:** feet and calves

### 5 · `sl_heel_raise_step` · Single-leg heel raise on a step (wk 3–4)
- **Setup:** right foot on a step edge (ball of the foot on the step, heel off), other foot lifted, fingertips on a wall.
- **Frames:** ① full height → ② heel lowered below step level. Tempo 3 s up · 3 s down.
- **Cues:** Full height at the top · Lower below the step · Drive through the big toe
- **Don't:** rolling to the outside · bouncing at the bottom
- **View:** side · **Highlight:** right foot and calf
- **Ball squeeze → big-toe drive** (agreed 14 Oct): the squeeze can't be done on one leg, so the inversion bias comes from the cue "drive through the big toe, heel tracks slightly in".

### 6 · `banded_inversion` · Banded foot inversion
- **Setup:** sitting, right leg crossed over the left knee, band around the forefoot, anchored to the **outside** (e.g. a table leg on your right).
- **Frames:** ① foot turned out by the band → ② foot swept in and up, sole turning toward you.
- **Cues:** Move only the foot · Sweep in and up · Control the way back
- **Don't:** rotating the whole leg
- **View:** front of the seated figure, plus a top-down foot inset with the in-and-up arrow · **Highlight:** right foot and inner shin
- Tempo isn't specified in the program; the proposal is controlled, about 2 s each way.

### 7 · `sl_heel_hold_towel` · Single-leg heel-raised hold on a towel
- **Setup:** standing on the right foot on a folded towel or cushion, heel raised, other foot lifted, fingertips on a wall (agreed 14 Oct).
- **Frames:** ① single frame, hold 30 s.
- **Cues:** Heel high · Ankle doesn't roll out · Breathe
- **Don't:** rolling to the outside · gripping the support
- **View:** side · **Highlight:** right foot and calf

## Test

### 8 · `sl_heel_raise_test` · Single-leg heel raises to fatigue
- **Setup:** standing on one foot on the flat floor, fingertips on a wall for balance only. Left then right.
- **Frames:** ① flat → ② full height. Tempo **2 s up · 2 s down** (agreed 14 Oct), the same for baseline (16 Oct) and retest (12 Nov).
- **Stop when:** you can't reach full height, you lose the tempo, or pain goes above 3/10. Count reps per side.
- **Cues:** Full height every rep · Same tempo · Fingertips only
- **Don't:** bending the knee · leaning on the wall
- **View:** side · **Highlight:** working foot and calf
